# NeRF Model Comparison

## Model Performance Comparison

| Model Type | Average PSNR (dB) | Average SSIM | Average LPIPS |
|------------|------------------|--------------|---------------|
| base | 1.54 | 0.0687 | 0.5425 |
| feature_conditioning | 1.56 | 0.0798 | 0.5222 |
| transformer | 1.57 | 0.0777 | 0.5220 |


Based on PSNR measurements, the **transformer** approach performs best with 1.57 dB.

Based on SSIM measurements, the **feature_conditioning** approach performs best with 0.0798.

Based on LPIPS measurements, the **transformer** and **feature_conditioning** approach performs best with 0.5220 (lower is better).
